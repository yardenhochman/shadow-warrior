# Sensor station hardware (perfboard + box)

Target: ESP32-WROOM 38-pin devkit, 2x ICS-43434 I2S mics (130 mm apart), LD2410 radar,
on a 5 x 7 cm perfboard in one box. Firmware pins are in `README.md`; this file is the
build spec. Pin positions below are for the common 38-pin devkit (DevKitC / NodeMCU-32S
clones): **check them against the silkscreen on your board before soldering**.

Diagram: `docs/wiring.svg` (generated by `docs/make_wiring.py`; open it in a browser).
Perfboard, hole by hole: `docs/perfboard.svg` and the build sheet `docs/PERFBOARD.md` (BOM, hole map, wire
list, assembly order with meter checks), both generated and validated by `uv run docs/make_perfboard.py`.
Box: `enclosure/box.py` (CadQuery, `uv run enclosure/box.py`) writes `enclosure/out/body.stl`,
`lid.stl` and `station.step`; `enclosure/preview.py` renders PNG previews.

## Layout in the box

```
 front panel (faces the arena)
 +------------------------------------------------------------+
 | [mic A, SEL=GND]           [ radar ]           [mic B, SEL=3V3] |
 |  port hole + gasket     antenna face to wall     port hole + gasket
 +------------------------------------------------------------+
        ~8-10 cm cable            |                 ~8-10 cm cable
                           [ perfboard 5x7 ]
                           [ devkit on female headers, USB at side wall ]
```

- Mic centres 130.0 mm apart (firmware `d_mm`). Mic breakouts glued face-down on the inside
  of the front panel, port over a 1.5-2 mm hole, thin foam gasket around the port.
- Radar stands upright, antenna face against the panel (<= 2 mm plastic, or a window).
- Radar and mic line axes agree: 0 deg = straight out of the front panel.
- WROOM antenna end overhangs the perfboard edge; no copper or metal near it.
- Radar must not point at the bag if the bag is in range (see bead ll3 notes).

## Pins (38-pin devkit, USB at the bottom)

| Left header (top to bottom) | Right header (top to bottom) |
|---|---|
| 3V3, EN, 36, 39, 34, 35, 32, **33**, **25**, **26**, **27**, 14, 12, GND, 13, 9, 10, 11, 5V | GND, 23, 22, TX0, RX0, 21, GND, 19, 18, 5, **17**, **16**, 4, 0, 2, 15, 8, 7, 6 |

Mics use the left header (3V3 at the top, 33/25/26 in the middle); the radar UART is on the
right header, 5V at the bottom left. Strapping pins 0, 2, 12, 15 are unused. This assumes a
WROOM module (a WROVER uses GPIO16/17 for PSRAM).

## Netlist

| Net | From | To |
|---|---|---|
| VMIC | devkit 3V3 -> R3a 100 ohm in parallel with R3b 100 ohm (50 ohm) | C1 10 uF + C2 100 nF to GND; J_MIC_A pin 1; J_MIC_B pin 1 |
| BCLK | GPIO26 (R1 33 ohm not fitted) | J_MIC_A pin 3; J_MIC_B pin 3 |
| LRCL | GPIO25 (R2 33 ohm not fitted) | J_MIC_A pin 4; J_MIC_B pin 4 |
| DOUT | J_MIC_A pin 5 and J_MIC_B pin 5 joined | GPIO33 (no series part) |
| GND (mics) | J_MIC_A/B pins 2 and 6 | star ground point |
| 5V_RADAR | devkit 5V pin | J_RADAR VCC; C3 470 uF + C4 100 nF to GND |
| RADAR_TX | J_RADAR TX | GPIO16 (ESP RX) |
| RADAR_RX | J_RADAR RX | GPIO17 (ESP TX) |
| RADAR_OUT | J_RADAR OUT | GPIO27 (optional; not wired on the perfboard) |
| GND (radar) | J_RADAR GND | star ground point (own wire, not shared with mic returns) |

Wiring by net, not by pin order: use single Dupont wires (or reorder the housings) so each wire lands
on the breakout pin with the same label; the header order on the breakout does not matter, only the net.
SEL does not go through the cable: on each breakout, tie SEL to a pin of the right net with a short jumper
wire or solder bridge: mic A SEL to its GND (left), mic B SEL to its 3V (right). Check with
`just direction` that positive angle is toward mic B; if reversed, swap the two SEL ties.

Mic cable (6 wires, same on both sides): 1 VMIC, 2 GND, 3 BCLK, 4 LRCL, 5 DOUT, 6 GND.
Twist 2+3 (GND with BCLK) and 5+6 (DOUT with GND); LRCL runs beside them. SEL is wired at the
mic end, not through the cable: mic A SEL to its GND (left), mic B SEL to its 3V (right).
Put 100 nF across 3V/GND on each mic breakout. Cable lengths (female-female Dupont): mic B (right, J1 is
on its side) 10 cm, mic A (left, across the box, ~12 cm straight) 20 cm folded flat along the back wall,
radar 10-15 cm. Equal length is not needed: BCLK is ~3 MHz (160 ns/bit), a 10 cm difference is ~0.5 ns.
Estimates from the layout, re-check once the box is printed.

## Parts

- ESP32 38-pin devkit + 2x 19-pin female headers
- 2x ICS-43434 breakouts (3V, GND, BCLK, DOUT, LRCL, SEL), LD2410 module (TX RX OUT GND VCC)
- R3a, R3b: 100 ohm in parallel (= 50 ohm; 22 ohm was the first plan, 100 ohm is what is in stock), 1/4 W
- R1, R2 (33 ohm series on BCLK/LRCL): not fitted, breadboard tests with long wires worked without them
- C1: 10 uF (ceramic or small electrolytic); C2, C4 + 2 at the mics: 100 nF; C3: 470 uF (radar 5 V bulk, >= 10 V, mind polarity)
- J_MIC: one 2x6 male pin header, a 1x6 Dupont housing per mic in each row (same nets column by column);
  J_RADAR: 1x5 male pin header, radar order TX RX OUT GND VCC (OUT not connected)
- Foam gasket, M2/M2.5 standoffs for the perfboard, 3D printed or plastic box

## Perfboard notes

- Board: PY-5CM*7CM single-sided, 24 x 18 isolated pads (silkscreen columns A..X, rows 01..18, on the
  copper side), four mounting holes about 3 mm in from the corners.
- Layout: `docs/PERFBOARD.md` / `docs/perfboard.svg`. Devkit sockets in rows 04 and 15, columns F..X (USB
  at X), antenna keep-out in columns A-E. J_MIC in L17..Q18 (pin 1 = column Q), mic filter and caps along
  rows 17-18, J_RADAR in O01..S01 with C3/C4 beside it. Nothing sits under the devkit.
- One star-ground pad (S16, next to the devkit GND pin); mic returns and the radar return reach it on
  separate wires.

## Measured parts (photos with a tape, +-1 mm; re-check with calipers)

- Devkit: ESP32-DevKitC clone, WROOM-32D, micro-USB. Pinout confirmed against the silkscreen
  (left 3V3..5V, right GND..CLK, as in the table above). PCB about 50 x 31.5 mm, module antenna
  overhangs about 6 mm. Header row spacing about 28.6 mm, which is 11 holes (27.94 mm):
  put the two female headers 11 holes apart and test fit before soldering.
  Micro-USB stands about 3 mm above the PCB, centred across the width.
- Mic breakout: about 16.5 x 12 mm, header along the long edge, bottom-port mic (PCB hole about
  1.3 mm, about 0.7 mm from centre, on the face opposite the header).
- Radar: HLK-LD2410C V1.1, about 21 x 16 mm. Antenna patches and the TX/RX/OUT/GND/VCC
  labels on the front; the header pins point out of the back.

## Enclosure (enclosure/box.py)

Two printed parts: a front shell (front panel + side walls, open at the back) and a lid.
Outside size about 161 x 61 x 34 mm; interior 156 x 56 x 32. Mic hole centres at x = -65 and
+65 mm (130.0 mm pitch), radar window at the centre, USB slot in the +x wall, four M3 corner posts.

- The values marked MEASURE at the top of `box.py` are assumptions (mic board 17 x 12.5 mm (photo estimate 16.5 x 12),
  radar 22 x 16 mm, port at the board centre, USB slot 13 x 9 mm, perfboard corner holes 3 mm in).
  Measure your parts and edit them before printing; reprint a test panel first.
- Print the shell front-face-down and the lid plate-down. PLA or PETG, 0.2 mm layers, no supports.
- Mics: glue each breakout face-down in its frame on the inside of the front panel, a 1 mm foam
  gasket around the port, port over the 2.4 mm hole (countersunk outside). Seal the gap; a leak
  lets internal reflections in. Keep the hole short (2 mm panel) so there is no resonance.
- Radar: antenna face against the panel behind the 1.2 mm window, header edge at the bottom notch.
- Perfboard: on the four lid standoffs, devkit USB end flush with the right edge, so the USB plug
  meets the slot. Screws: M3 x 8 self tapping through the lid into the posts.
