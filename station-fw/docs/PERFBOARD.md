`uv run docs/make_perfboard.py`, never this file). Drawing: [`perfboard.svg`](perfboard.svg). Schematic:

## Board and conventions

- PY-5CM*7CM single-sided perfboard, 24 columns x 18 rows of isolated round pads at 2.54 mm, four
  mounting holes about 3 mm in from the corners (they sit outside the pad grid at the left and right ends).
- A hole is named by the silkscreen: column letter + row, e.g. **N15** = column 14, row 15. Tables also
  give (col,row) with col 1..24 = A..X.
- The drawing shows the **component side** (column A on the left, row 01 at the top). The silkscreen and the
  copper are on the **other side**, where the board is **mirrored left-right** (X..A from left to right in
  your photo). Rows do not change. Always find holes by their printed name.
- All bridges, bare links and insulated wires are on the **copper side**. Parts and the devkit sockets are
  on the component side.
- Connection types: **bridge** = solder blob between two ADJACENT pads of the same net; **link** = bare tinned
  wire (a cut resistor lead) laid straight on the pads and soldered at every pad it crosses; **wire** = insulated
  30 AWG wire-wrap (or thin hookup) wire, stripped 2-3 mm at each end. GND: the two GND rails (L2, and the
  bridges S15-S16-S17) are bare; every longer GND run (W3, W4, W9, W10, W11) is insulated.
- Antenna keep-out: columns A-E, rows 03-16 stay empty (the WROOM antenna overhangs there).
- Nothing is under the devkit (rows 05-14). Tall parts (C1, C3, headers) are only in rows 01-02 and 17-18.

## Order of assembly (measure after every step, devkit NOT plugged in)

Meter on continuity (beep). "Beep" pairs must beep; "no beep" pairs must not. Hole names are printed on
the copper side, where you probe.

### 1. Devkit sockets

Plug both 19-pin female headers onto the devkit, put them through rows 04 and 15, columns F..X (F04 and F15 are pin 1 at the antenna end, X04/X15 at the USB end). Check from the component side that the USB end is toward column X. Solder the four end pins, unplug the devkit (heat), then solder the rest.

- No beep: S15-R15, S15-T15, X15-W15, F15-G15, P04-Q04

### 2. Star ground

Solder blobs S15-S16 and S16-S17. S16 is the star: later W4 (mics) and W11 (radar) end here. Keep R16/T16 clean.

- Beep: S15-S17 (GND)
- No beep: S16-R15, S16-T15, S16-R16, S16-T16

### 3. Mic supply filter

R3a F17-J17 and R3b F18-J18 lying flat. Bridge F17-F18 and J17-J18. Wire W1 F15 to F17 (the devkit 3V3 pin).

- Beep: F15-F18 (3V3)
- No beep: F15-G15, F15-S15

### 4. Mic header and I2S

J1 (2x6 male) in L17..Q18, short pins on the copper side. Bridge each column L..Q from row 17 to row 18 and nothing sideways. Wires W5 M15-M17 (GPIO33 DOUT), W6 N15-N17 (GPIO25 LRCL), W7 O15-O17 (GPIO26 BCLK).

- Beep: M15-M18 (DOUT), N15-N18 (LRCL), O15-O18 (BCLK)
- No beep: L17-M17, M17-N17, N17-O17, O17-P17, P17-Q17, L18-M18, P18-Q18, M15-N15, N15-O15

### 5. Mic decoupling, rails, test pins

C2 R17/R18, C1 T17 (-) / T18 (+): the stripe (-) toward row 17. TP1 (2 pins) V17 GND / V18 VMIC. Bare links L1 along row 18 Q18..V18 and L2 along row 17 R17..V17: lay them straight, solder at every pad, then check the gap between the two rows along its whole length. Wires W2 (VMIC, outer margin), W3 (GND, inner margin), W4.

- Beep: J18-Q17 (VMIC), J18-V18 (VMIC), L17-S15 (GND), P17-S16 (GND), V17-S15 (GND)
- No beep: V17-V18, Q17-R17, S17-S18, F15-S15
- C1 polarity: the stripe (minus) is on T17, the GND rail; the long lead (+) in T18, the VMIC rail.

### 6. Radar connector, bulk cap, 5 V

J2 (1x5 male) O01..S01, pin 1 (TX) at O01. C4 T01/T02. C3 V01 (+) / V02 (-): stripe toward row 02. Bare link L3 along row 01 S01..W01. W8 from the devkit 5V pin X15 along the right edge (outside the pads) to W01. W9 R01-T02, W10 T02-V02, W11 V02 to the star S16 (between the header pins W/X). Radar GND has its own wire to the star.

- Beep: X15-S01 (5V), X15-V01 (5V), R01-S16 (GND), V02-S15 (GND)
- No beep: S01-R01, T01-T02, V01-V02, X15-S15
- C3 polarity: the stripe (minus) is on V02 (GND); + in V01 on the 5 V link. A reversed 470 uF can vent.

### 7. Radar UART

W12 P01 to P04 (J2 RX to GPIO17), W13 O01 to Q04 (J2 TX to GPIO16). They cross once: both insulated.

- Beep: P01-P04 (RADAR_RX), O01-Q04 (RADAR_TX)
- No beep: O01-P01, P01-Q01, P04-Q04, O01-P04

### 8. Final checks before any USB

Devkit unplugged, no mic or radar cable. Ohms range for the resistor line, continuity for the rest:

| Between | Holes | Expect |
|---|---|---|
| 3V3 - GND | F15 - S15 | open (no beep) |
| VMIC - GND | V18 - V17 | starts low, climbs (C1 charging), ends > 10 kohm; never a steady 0 |
| 5V - GND | X15 - S15 | starts low, climbs (C3 470 uF charges slowly), ends > 10 kohm |
| 3V3 - VMIC | F15 - V18 | 50 ohm (R3a and R3b in parallel); 100 ohm means one is open |
| 3V3 - 5V | F15 - X15 | open |
| VMIC - 5V | V18 - X15 | open |
| BCLK - GND | O15 - S15 | open |
| LRCL - GND | N15 - S15 | open |
| DOUT - GND | M15 - S15 | open |
| BCLK - LRCL | O15 - N15 | open |
| LRCL - DOUT | N15 - M15 | open |
| RADAR_TX - RADAR_RX | Q04 - P04 | open |
| RADAR_TX - GND | Q04 - S15 | open |
| RADAR_RX - 5V | P04 - X15 | open |

Also look at the copper side under a light: every bridge between the right two pads, nothing between the J1
columns sideways, nothing between rows 17 and 18 along L1/L2, no whiskers at the header pins next to W11 (W/X).

### 9. First power-up

1. Plug the devkit in (USB toward column X, check pin F15 = 3V3 on the devkit silkscreen). No mic, no radar.
   USB from a current-limited port or a USB meter if you have one: idle draw about 40-80 mA.
2. Measure DC to GND (S15 or V17): F15 3.3 V, V18 (TP1 VMIC) 3.2-3.3 V, X15 / V01 4.7-5.1 V.
3. Unplug USB. Plug the radar cable (TX to O01, pin 1). Power: 5 V at V01 still >= 4.7 V; radar LED / UART
   frames in the firmware log.
4. Unplug USB. Plug both mic cables (pin 1 to column Q). Power: VMIC (V18) still about 3.25 V; run the
   firmware level check (README) and tap each mic: A must show in the left slot, B in the right.
